# Lint the wiki

1. Go through `Schema/lint-checklist.md` item by item.
2. Follow the **Lint** steps in `Schema/workflows.md`.
3. Fix only small, safe problems yourself. Ask before deleting, merging or renaming pages.
4. Report what you fixed and list what needs a decision.
