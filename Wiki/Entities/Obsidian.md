# Obsidian

## Who or what it is

Obsidian is a note-taking app. In the source it is the tool used to build a [[Knowledge Graph]]: each note is a node, and putting a word in double square brackets creates a link (an edge) to another note. Its graph view shows the notes and links as a map.

The source also mentions the Obsidian Web Clipper as a way to bring articles and research into a vault as raw sources.
