# Wanderloots

## Who or what it is

Wanderloots is the YouTube channel and author of the source video on why to build an [[LLM Wiki]]. In the video he introduces himself as Callum, a former intellectual property lawyer and patent agent who left that job three years earlier and has built his personal knowledge system in [[Obsidian]] since then.

He keeps two vaults: a human vault for his own thinking and an LLM vault for AI-generated notes. See [[Agentic Vault]].
