# Andrej Karpathy

## Who or what it is

Andrej Karpathy is the author of the post that made the [[LLM Wiki]] idea popular. The source describes him as formerly of OpenAI and as the person who coined the term "vibe coding".

His framing, as the source reports it: instead of retrieving from raw documents at query time, the LLM incrementally builds and maintains a persistent wiki of interlinked Markdown files. See [[Compiled Knowledge]].
